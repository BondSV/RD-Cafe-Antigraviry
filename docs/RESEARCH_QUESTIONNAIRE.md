# RainyDay Café post-session questionnaire

Instrument version: 2026-10-27-v1. Target completion time: approximately five minutes. Offered once after an explicit **Finish playing** action covering the whole playing session, regardless of the number of attempts. Do not show it after each run or between **Play Again** attempts. The questionnaire is optional, has separate affirmative research consent, and individual questions may be left unanswered. Skip questionnaire is available only before agreement and opening the questions. The opened form has one action, Submit questionnaire, and requires at least one answer. No substantive response is counted for a skipped or entirely blank form.

Instructions: Think about the whole playing session, including all your attempts, rather than only your last run. Your answers are not assessed. Please do not include anyone's name, student number or other identifying details.

1. Describe a decision you made and what you expected would happen. What happened instead, if anything?
2. Describe an outcome that surprised you, if there was one. What do you think caused it?
3. What, if anything, did you change about your approach as you played? What prompted that change?
4. What information did you use when choosing your actions? Describe how it influenced a particular decision.
5. Was there anything you found difficult to explain, confusing or unhelpful? Please describe it. You may add anything else about your experience here.

Answers are limited to 2,000 characters each. Do not record keystrokes or send draft prose to gameplay telemetry. Preserve a consented draft locally for recovery, submit through a dedicated authenticated endpoint, use a stable response identifier for idempotent retries and show success only after server acknowledgement. Researcher exports must retain instrument/session/condition/assignment-basis metadata, distinguish respondents from runs and protect CSV text against formula injection.

Questionnaire consent is separate from gameplay permission. Explain any link to recorded gameplay explicitly. A questionnaire-only respondent has an independent random credential and no fabricated gameplay record or retrospectively uploaded unrecorded runs. Interview recruitment/contact details remain outside this form. Complete the questionnaire before the common tutor debrief where operationally possible; the app must not claim to observe an unrecorded debrief.
