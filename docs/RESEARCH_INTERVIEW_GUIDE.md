# RainyDay Café student interview guide

Version: 2026-10-27-v1. Approximately 45 minutes. Up to 16 student interviews across the whole study, with a target of eight from each condition. This is separate from the approximately five-minute questionnaire offered after the whole playing session. Gameplay and questionnaire recruitment are not capped at 16 students.

This draft follows the signed forms’ voluntary interview and withdrawal provisions. Sergii has confirmed that live interviews remain opt-in while classroom gameplay data should always be collected. The proposed always-on gameplay recording and research use remain unreconciled with the signed voluntary-consent forms; interview consent does not authorise that separate collection. This guide does not establish approval or silently change the signed commitments.

## Selection and preparation

The non-teaching researcher selects students across conditions, groups/intakes and contrasting patterns of engagement. Include limited progress, confusion and unresolved explanations, not only high scores. Multiple attempts from one student are not independent participants. Prepare one or two moments from that student's recorded runs. A questionnaire response, where separately consented and linked, may suggest a useful follow-up but is not an interview prerequisite.

The game holds no general student-to-code lookup table. Only an interview volunteer who chooses to share their code creates an identity-to-gameplay link with the interviewer. Do not create a universal mapping. Keep recruitment/contact details and that limited volunteer mapping outside the gameplay/questionnaire dataset, separately with the interviewer in the approved research location and with restricted access. Confirm actual permissions before using a replay or questionnaire answer in an interview. Record the interview date as a later research contribution for the withdrawal-window calculation. The software cannot observe the interview itself.

## Opening, approximately 5 minutes

Confirm voluntary interview participation and explicitly ask whether the student agrees to share their study code and link their existing gameplay records to this interview. Obtain separate permission for audio recording and explain the route for requesting withdrawal. Interview or audio consent alone does not authorise that linkage or the independent collection/research use of the wider gameplay dataset. Explain that the interview is not an assessment and there is no expected correct explanation. Invite them to decline a question, pause, or ask to remove a contribution.

“What was your overall approach to playing the game across your attempts?”

## Their decisions and feedback, approximately 20 minutes

Invite the student to choose a run or moment they remember. Use the recorded decision and feedback as a prompt:

- What were you trying to achieve at this point?
- What did you expect would happen at the time?
- What information did you use when deciding?
- What happened, and how did you interpret it?
- Was anything different from what you expected? What did you make of that?

Invite uncertainty and distinguish recollection from later interpretation. Do not teach a correct explanation, repair their account or infer their thinking from a score or duration. Replay reconstructs decisions and feedback, not exact customer animation, attention or original thought.

## Later decisions and repeated runs, approximately 10 minutes

Choose a later decision or a subsequent attempt:

- Did your approach change as you played? What prompted that change?
- Were there outcomes you could not explain?
- Did you return to an earlier idea or try an alternative? What led you to do that?

Where permitted and available, ask neutrally about a specific questionnaire answer: “You mentioned this experience. Would you like to say more about it?” Do not require consistency with that earlier answer.

## Information and closing, approximately 10 minutes

Use wording appropriate to the version actually experienced:

- How did the information shown before you committed, or the feedback shown afterwards, enter into your decisions?
- Was anything confusing, difficult to explain or unhelpful?
- Is there anything else about your experience you would like to add?

Avoid assuming one version is better or that playing produced learning. Clarify when a reflection relates to gameplay, the later tutor debrief or the interview itself. Close with the withdrawal route, any requested removal and how the eventual study summary will be made available.

## Withdrawal process

The proposed forms allow at least 14 calendar days after the participant's final research contribution. A later questionnaire or interview can extend that date. The researcher must maintain the final-contribution date separately and check it before determining any deadline. Do not calculate the deadline only from gameplay or destroy the linking material automatically after 14 days. Apply an accepted full-study withdrawal to the protected app records and available server backups, recruitment/contact mappings, interview material and all previously downloaded exports. The app does not establish institutional approval or a general retention period.
