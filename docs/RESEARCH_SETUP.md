## Student flow

1. Use the link for the actual classroom session. The current local candidate requires recording during an open classroom session, shows its actual label and recording information, and has no opt-in checkbox or unrecorded-play route during that session. A connection failure cannot silently enter unrecorded play. This is software capability, not approval of compulsory research.
2. Students play individually for the planned 45–60 minutes. Both conditions select a card and confirm it; only the prediction condition sees consequences before commitment. Existing deterministic rules, seven turns, artwork, score and matched confirmation flow remain. Every turn, including the seventh, displays feedback before the final summary.
3. A run is one attempt. **Play Again** begins another run under the same code and condition. The questionnaire never appears merely because a run ends, a tab loses focus, a refresh occurs or a connection fails. Refresh starts another attempt and preserves earlier logged attempts as incomplete rather than resuming them.
4. **Finish playing** is available in the study bar during an incomplete run and on the final summary. It asks for deliberate confirmation. It freezes research gameplay for that participant/session, retains all earlier completed/incomplete/restarted runs and saves an explicit finish context. Queued game records remain recoverable. Finish acknowledgement waits for preceding gameplay records; it does not turn an upload failure into a completed session.
5. Only then is the approximately five-minute questionnaire offered. It concerns the whole playing session. It has separate, unchecked consent and explains any linkage to recorded runs. Gameplay permission is not questionnaire permission. All five questions are individually optional. Skip and blank forms are not substantive responses. No names or student numbers are requested. The exact wording and version are in `RESEARCH_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`.
6. Submit success appears only after server acknowledgement. A failed/offline submission retains a consented local draft and stable submission identifier for retry. Keystrokes and draft prose are not sent to gameplay telemetry. The app stores questionnaire prose only through its dedicated endpoint, with 2,000 characters per answer. JSON preserves exact answers; CSV prefixes potential spreadsheet formulas with an apostrophe.
7. After submitting or skipping, a student can continue with clearly unrecorded practice. This does not reopen the completed research gameplay phase or re-offer the questionnaire between subsequent practice attempts. Arrange questionnaire completion before the common tutor debrief. The app does not claim to observe the debrief.
8. Ask interview volunteers to retain their study/reference code. Interview recruitment/contact details and identity mapping stay outside gameplay/questionnaire answers. The student interview guide is `RESEARCH_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`. Up to 16 approximately 45-minute student interviews across the study is separate from broad gameplay/questionnaire sampling and from Harish's additional designer interview with Sergii. No arbitrary total gameplay/questionnaire student cap is imposed.

## Independent questionnaire participation and interpretation

The dedicated respondent model supports questionnaire-only participation with an independent credential, a null gameplay link and `assignment_basis=unrecorded_practice`. It never uploads earlier unrecorded play or fabricates runs. Such respondents are labelled as non-randomised and are not pooled with the randomised control condition. In the current required-recording candidate, the in-classroom unrecorded-play choice is intentionally absent for required sessions, while independently configured voluntary sessions support that path. That policy conflicts with the signed forms and must be reconciled explicitly, rather than claiming the forms' independent voluntary gameplay path has been implemented under a compulsory policy.

A questionnaire is unique per linked participant/classroom session, or per independent respondent credential. Several runs remain linked observations from one person, not several student observations. The dashboard and exports count participants, runs, consented respondents and submitted questionnaires separately. A lost browser credential or unfinished questionnaire is not labelled completed. Geometry/timing records establish on-screen exposure and elapsed time, not reading, attention, reasons or learning. Interviews are retrospective recollections. Identical intervention sets yield identical final rule-based indicators, although intermediate experiences can differ. Replay reconstructs decisions and feedback, not the exact stochastic customer animation.

## Saved data, access and removal

Gameplay events retain immutable IDs, participant/run/page references, sequence, browser elapsed/client time, server receipt time, canonical state, version and allowlisted interaction data. The event stream continues to reject arbitrary prose. Questionnaire responses are separate records with respondent/optional participant references, classroom metadata, actual condition/assignment basis, game version, consent/instrument versions, timestamps, stable submission ID and validated answers. Submission retries are idempotent; another respondent cannot read or overwrite a response.

The researcher can filter classroom sessions, replay gameplay, inspect questionnaire answers and download full JSON plus decisions/events/questionnaire CSV. Exported credentials are excluded. Researcher deletion of a linked participant removes relevant gameplay, session finish and questionnaire records. A questionnaire-only respondent can also be found and withdrawn. Primary and available server backups are purged, with a minimal tombstone to reject late uploads. Queued local material can only be cleared when that device reconnects and receives the withdrawal response; do not claim remote deletion instantly erases an offline browser. Previously downloaded exports, interview material and contact mappings require a separate researcher deletion process.

The forms promise at least 14 calendar days after the final research contribution. The software imposes no automatic 14-day deletion-right expiry. A later questionnaire or externally conducted interview can extend the window; the lead researcher must maintain the actual final-contribution date outside the app, retain linking material until all applicable windows end and apply accepted requests. No general research-data retention duration has been invented.
